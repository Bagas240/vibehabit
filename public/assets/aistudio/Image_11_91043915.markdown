## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a playful, comforting, and grounded environment for Gen Z habit tracking. It discards harsh electric neons, high-stress visual alerts, and clinical minimalism in favor of an approachable, tactile "cartoonish minimalist" aesthetic—reminiscent of high-grade matte vinyl stickers, indie editorial zines, and tactile paper stationery.

Key aesthetic attributes:
- **Zero Gradients:** Every surface and element relies strictly on 100% solid, opaque, flat fills to create a clean, modern poster-print feel.
- **Eye-Friendly Softness:** Eliminates pitch blacks and blinding stark whites. All surfaces lean into creamy milk tones and muted slate mists to prevent visual fatigue during habitual daily tracking.
- **Playful Tactility:** Generously rounded corners and purposeful solid ink outlines give components a physical, collectible sticker presence.
- **Friendly Composure:** Tone remains encouraging, relaxed, and non-judgmental, emphasizing daily momentum rather than algorithmic gamification stress.

## Colors

The palette operates entirely on flat, matte tones calibrated for prolonged screen exposure and high legibility without fluorescent fatigue.

- **Primary (`#3B68B5` - Dusty Denim Blue):** A calm, saturated yet muted cobalt used for primary buttons, active tracker nodes, and key completion states.
- **Secondary (`#8CA6DB` - Muted Periwinkle):** A friendly, soft pastel accent used for streaks, secondary highlights, tags, and category icons.
- **Tertiary (`#98B6A2` - Soft Pastel Sage):** A restorative, herbal pastel green used for success confirmations, rest days, completed checkmarks, and supportive positive states.
- **Neutral Core (`#1E2530` - Deep Muted Ink):** Replaces pitch black (`#000000`). Used for typography, iconography, and structured sticker-style outlines.
- **Canvas (`#ECEFF5` - Creamy Mist):** A pale, desaturated slate background that softens the screen viewport.
- **Surface (`#FAFAFC` - Milk Card):** A soft, warm off-white reserved for cards, sheets, and modular containers to maintain contrast against the canvas without blinding glare.

## Typography

The design system exclusively adopts **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. Its geometric balance, rounded terminals, and friendly apertures directly harmonize with the cartoonish minimalist theme without sacrificing legibility or feeling overly childish.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in bold and extrabold weights with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`), generating punchy, confident title anchors.
- **Body:** Kept at medium (`500`) weight rather than normal (`400`) to provide solid ink balance against soft pastel surfaces and ensure text never feels washed out.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered with elevated weights (`600` and `700`) and slight letter tracking to deliver crisp utility tags, streak counters, and button text.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid, responsive structure framed inside fixed comfortable container maximums (max width `480px` for mobile app views, `960px` for wide tablet dashboards).

- **Grid Structure:** A 4-column system on mobile devices and an 8-column layout on tablet/desktop. 
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a modular 4px/8px rhythm. Compact spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) separates micro-elements such as streak day dots and habit labels, while larger spacing (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) isolates discrete habit categories and date columns.
- **Responsiveness:** Gutter sizes contract from `1rem` down to `0.75rem` on small viewports to maintain wide card canvases, ensuring habit titles remain visible without aggressive truncation.

## Elevation & Depth

To stay faithful to the matte cartoonish aesthetic and strictly avoid artificial glow or blurred lighting:

- **No Blurred Shadows:** Diffused, atmospheric box-shadows are entirely forbidden.
- **Tactile Solid Offsets (Sticker Depth):** Depth is created exclusively via a crisp, solid 2px ink outline (`#1E2530`) combined optionally with an unblurred hard-edge drop fill (`2px 2px 0px #1E2530` or `3px 3px 0px #1E2530`).
- **Surface Nesting:** Visual separation between the viewport and actionable items relies on solid contrast: the muted canvas (`#ECEFF5`) holds the bright milk cards (`#FAFAFC`), which in turn hold soft pastel chips and indicators (`#8CA6DB`, `#98B6A2`).
- **Active / Pressed Physics:** Interactive items depress visually on tap, shifting position (`translate(2px, 2px)`) while shedding their hard-edge offset, mimicking a physical click button.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary revolves around chunky, friendly, and protective forms:
- **Base Rounding (`roundedness: 2`):** Primary interactive blocks, cards, and modal dialogs use `0.5rem` to `1rem` corner rounding.
- **Pill Accents:** Frequency pills, streak badges, and floating status chips use full pill rounding (`9999px`) to emphasize a smooth sticker quality.
- **Outlines:** Key interactive surfaces and badges feature uniform solid ink borders (`1.5px` to `2px` width in `#1E2530`), reinforcing the cartoon-like separation of elements without feeling heavy.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid dusty denim blue (`#3B68B5`) fill, milk white text (`#FAFAFC`), solid ink border (`1.5px solid #1E2530`), with a subtle hard-edge offset (`2px 2px 0px #1E2530`). On press, translates `2px` down-right with offset removed.
- **Secondary Action:** Milk card fill (`#FAFAFC`), deep muted ink text (`#1E2530`), solid ink border (`1.5px solid #1E2530`).
- **Destructive / Reset:** Solid pastel coral tint with ink border and text.

### Habit Cards & Tracking Rows
- **Surface:** Solid milk card (`#FAFAFC`) with a crisp outline (`1.5px solid #1E2530`) and rounded corners (`1rem`).
- **Layout:** Habit title on the left with custom category icon pill; horizontal 7-day completion bubble strip on the right.
- **Interaction:** Cards have zero hover blur; cards visually raise with a 2px hard solid drop on hover on desktop devices.

### Completion Bubbles (Checkboxes)
- **Unchecked State:** Circular or soft-rounded square (`0.5rem`), creamy mist fill (`#ECEFF5`), outline (`1.5px solid #1E2530`).
- **Completed State:** Pops to solid pastel sage (`#98B6A2`) or dusty denim (`#3B68B5`) with a bold ink checkmark icon (`#FAFAFC` or `#1E2530`). Instant, snappy spring animation.

### Chips & Badges
- **Streak Counters:** Pill shape, soft muted periwinkle fill (`#8CA6DB`), deep muted ink text (`#1E2530`), solid ink outline (`1px solid #1E2530`).
- **Filter Tags:** Milk card background in default state; snaps to solid dusty denim blue with white text when selected.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Milk card (`#FAFAFC`) with an inset feel, framed by a `1.5px solid #1E2530` border.
- **Focus State:** Retains the crisp outline and introduces a solid `2px 2px 0px #8CA6DB` hard offset without any soft glow rings.
- **Placeholder:** Muted ink at 50% opacity.

### Streak / Progress Ring
- **Format:** Circular, thick track (`8px`) in creamy mist (`#ECEFF5`), completed segment filled with solid dusty denim (`#3B68B5`) or pastel sage (`#98B6A2`), terminating in clean rounded caps with no gradients.
